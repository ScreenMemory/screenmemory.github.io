How It Works

1. Set Your Interval

Choose how often to capture screenshots—every 10 seconds, 1 minute, or whatever fits your flow. Runs quietly in the background.

2. Browse Like Video

Open the player to scrub through time. Fast-forward, rewind, zoom into details across all your screens.

3. Search Instantly

Type in the textbox to filter by text on screen. Find websites, names, code, or interfaces from days or weeks ago.

4. Export or Pause

Grab frames, pause recording anytime, or move your library. Seamless across updates.

FAQ

Is it safe and private?

Screenshots stay local on your Mac. No cloud upload, no sharing—your history is yours alone.

How much space does it use?

Depends on interval and retention. A full day at 30-second intervals uses about 5-10 GB. Manage easily in settings.

Does it slow my Mac?

Optimized for macOS—minimal CPU and battery impact. Runs efficiently even on extended sessions.

Works on multiple screens?

Yes, captures all connected displays. Perfect for ultrawides or dual setups.
